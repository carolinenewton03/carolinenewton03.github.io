Project Overview

AI Job Market Dashboard

The AI industry is evolving rapidly, creating new career opportunities while changing the skills expected by employers.

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze AI job market trends, salary distributions, hiring patterns, and skill requirements.

The dashboard transforms raw job posting data into meaningful visual insights, enabling users to better understand the current landscape of AI careers and make more informed decisions.

Problem Statement

Understanding a Rapidly Changing Job Market

With the rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence, students and professionals often struggle to answer important career questions.

  • Which AI roles are currently most in demand?
  • What technical skills are employers actively seeking?
  • How do salaries differ across job categories?
  • Which areas of AI show the greatest growth potential?

Although large amounts of job market data exist, extracting actionable insights from raw information remains challenging. This project aims to bridge that gap through interactive analytics and visual storytelling.

Dataset & Preparation

From Public Data to Actionable Insights

The project uses a publicly available AI job market dataset sourced from Kaggle.

Rather than focusing on data collection, the primary objective was to transform existing job market information into meaningful analytical insights.

The dataset includes job roles, salary information, required skills, experience expectations, and employment-related characteristics.

Preprocessing was performed to improve data quality, standardize information, and prepare the dataset for interactive analysis within Power BI.

Workflow

From raw listings to career insights.

The dashboard development followed a typical analytics lifecycle, beginning with understanding the dataset and ending with interactive visual storytelling.

  • Data ExplorationReviewed job roles, salary fields, skills, and market attributes.
  • Data PreparationCleaned missing values, standardized categories, and transformed fields for analysis.
  • Analysis & KPIsCreated DAX measures and calculated metrics to capture hiring trends and salary patterns.
  • VisualizationDesigned interactive Power BI visuals to communicate market insights clearly.
  • StorytellingStructured the dashboard to guide users from overview metrics to deeper exploration.
Design Approach

Designing for Exploration and Decision-Making

The dashboard was designed to prioritize usability, clarity, and interactivity.

  • Interactive filters were added to support flexible exploration.
  • Dynamic KPI cards summarize important metrics at a glance.
  • Comparative visuals help analyze roles, salaries, and skill demand.
  • Visual hierarchy was used to improve readability and reduce clutter.
  • The layout was structured to guide users from overview to deeper analysis.

The objective was not simply to display charts, but to create an analytical tool capable of supporting data-driven decision-making.

Insights

Key Insights Discovered

Analysis of the dataset revealed several important trends across the AI job market.

Roles such as Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, and Data Analyst consistently appeared among the most frequently advertised positions, highlighting sustained demand for analytical and machine learning expertise.
Python emerged as one of the most commonly requested technical skills across job postings, reinforcing its position as a foundational programming language within the modern AI ecosystem.
Salary distributions varied considerably across job categories, suggesting that factors such as specialization, experience level, and role complexity significantly influence compensation patterns.
Cloud computing technologies, machine learning frameworks, and data engineering capabilities repeatedly appeared within job requirements, indicating increasing industry emphasis on scalable and production-ready AI solutions.
Overall hiring trends suggest continued expansion of AI-related opportunities across multiple industries, reflecting the growing adoption of artificial intelligence and data-driven technologies worldwide.
Features

Interactive Dashboard Capabilities

The dashboard is designed for interactive exploration rather than static reporting.

  • Filter job market data across multiple dimensions.
  • Compare salary trends across AI-related roles.
  • Identify frequently requested technical skills.
  • Explore role demand and hiring patterns visually.
  • Move from high-level KPIs to detailed observations.

These features allow users to explore the AI job market based on their own goals, whether they are comparing roles, planning skills, or reviewing market direction.

Applications

Transforming Data into Career Intelligence

The dashboard provides practical value for multiple stakeholders within the AI ecosystem.

StudentsStudents can identify industry-relevant skills, understand emerging career pathways, and align their learning journey with current market expectations.
Job SeekersJob seekers can benchmark salary expectations, compare different AI roles, and identify the technical competencies increasingly valued by employers.
Recruiters and Hiring TeamsRecruiters can gain broader insights into talent demand patterns, helping them better understand hiring trends and role requirements across AI domains.
Academic InstitutionsAcademic institutions can use labor market insights to evaluate whether existing curricula reflect evolving industry needs and technological advancements.

By transforming raw job market data into interactive and accessible visual insights, the dashboard enables more informed academic, professional, and workforce-related decisions.

Challenges & Learnings

Challenges Faced and Lessons Learned

Developing the dashboard involved technical, analytical, and design-related challenges.

The dataset contained missing values, inconsistent formats, and categorical differences that required preprocessing before meaningful analysis could be performed.

Standardizing salary information, job role categories, and skill descriptions was necessary to make comparisons more reliable across the dashboard.

Designing the report also required balancing detail with readability, so the visuals remained useful without overwhelming the user.

Through this project, I strengthened my skills in cleaning publicly available job market datasets, creating DAX measures, building KPI logic, designing interactive Power BI reports, and using data storytelling to convert raw information into clear career insights.

The project also improved my ability to think like an analyst: define useful questions, structure the data around those questions, and present findings in a way that supports decision-making.
